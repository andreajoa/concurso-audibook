# Trilha Aprova — Scroll Craft Brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation from the user.

## 1. Vibe
Premium editorial, disciplined, trustworthy, aspirational, conversion-focused.

References supplied by the user: the existing Trilha Aprova brand, its navy/gold/off-white identity, the 3D apostila mockup, and the requirement for a cutting-edge premium storefront. No outside visual reference was invented.

## 2. Journey
1. Recognition — visitor immediately sees a real apostila for a real concurso, not a generic education landing page.
2. Specificity — contest, bank, audience, page count, question count and formats become concrete.
3. Confidence — the page explains what the material solves and why the formats exist.
4. Method — the visitor sees a four-stage study route instead of a folder of downloads.
5. Security — payment and buyer-only access are explained before commitment.
6. Commitment — one purchase action: “Comprar esta apostila”.

## 3. Energy curve
- Object 01 / hero: focused confidence — the 3D cover dominates the first screen.
- Evidence: calm clarity — factual blocks explain what is inside and what problem each part solves.
- Signature route: rising momentum — the gold Trilha line advances through four study stages.
- Formats: relief — PDF and audio adapt to different moments of the day.
- Access: reassurance — protected delivery and payment flow are made explicit.
- Close: decisive — quiet catalog plate with the same purchase action.

## 4. Feeling curve and peak
Peak: the gold Trilha route advancing through Orientação → Contraste → Recuperação → Repetição.

The sentence a visitor should remember: “É o site em que a própria trilha mostra como eu usaria a apostila para estudar.”

Tell-someone sentence: “É a plataforma de apostilas em que cada concurso aparece como um objeto de catálogo e a Trilha Aprova mostra a rota de estudo pelo scroll.”

## 5. Signature move
**Trilha de Estudo** — a bespoke gold route whose fill and active station advance with scroll through the four intended study stages. It belongs to the Trilha Aprova brand and is not a generic progress bar.

## 6. Aesthetic range
Premium-editorial / catalog. Not maximalist, not playful, not brutalist. High contrast between paper, navy and champagne gold; strong typography; product object first.

## 7. Structural choice
Distinct scenes. Grammar: **Gallery / Catalog**.

Reason: the platform is intended to host multiple apostilas. The product object and its factual label are the natural page unit. This avoids a generic hero followed by product cards and makes the catalog itself the experience.

## 8. Existing assets
- Trilha Aprova logo: `public/assets/trilha-aprova-logo.webp`
- 3D apostila: `public/assets/apostila-santos-ibam-3d.webp`
- Existing Stripe checkout, CRM analytics, buyer access and recovery flows.

No KIE asset generation is used.

## Product / audience / belief / action
- Product: digital apostila + audiobook for Concurso Prefeitura de Santos • Banca IBAM.
- Audience: candidates preparing for Professor Adjunto I / II — Educação Especial.
- Belief to install: a well-structured, targeted material can reduce study dispersion and make revision more efficient, without falsely guaranteeing approval.
- One action: **Comprar esta apostila**.

## Authored silence
The space around the 3D cover and the quiet catalog close are intentional pauses, not dead scroll.
